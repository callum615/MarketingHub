## Insights
- WA volume/project home builders with display villages are almost always tied to a group-owned exclusive mortgage broker: ABN Group→Resolve Finance (Dale Alcock Homes, Celebration Homes, Homebuyers Centre, Webb & Brown-Neaves, Boutique Homes), Scott Park Group→Lets Finance/SPG Finance Pty Ltd (B1 Homes, Redink Homes), Endeavour Homes (formerly Gemmill Homes)→Central Lending Solutions. Ray White Group also owns Loan Market Group outright. Independent/no finance-arm found: Content Living, WOW Homes, Home Group WA (ambiguous, verify by phone), Century 21 Gold Key Realty, Professionals Northern Coast, Northern Beaches Realty, Blueprint Homes (unverified).
- At least two "financial planner" practices in the corridor (Luda Financial Solutions/Jacques Hugo, and Nathan Yap in Clarkson) also operate as mortgage brokers themselves — direct competitors, not referral partners. Always check for this dual-registration before adding a "financial planner" to an outreach list.
